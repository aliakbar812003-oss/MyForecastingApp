How it works

Understand what comes from the market, what is a model, and how to interpret each.

Market charts & quotes

TradingView supplies embedded charts, quote statistics, and technical analysis for the selected exchange symbol. US and European stocks use local listings. Crypto uses Coinbase USD pairs.

Quotes may be delayed, unavailable, or restricted for some exchanges. Check the widget’s timestamp and exchange notices. This app does not independently verify quotes.

Currencies & trading hours

US stocks are usually quoted in USD, euro-area listings in EUR, Swiss listings in CHF, Danish listings in DKK, and Swedish listings in SEK. UK shares are typically quoted in GBX (pence): 100 GBX equals £1.

Stock exchanges have local trading hours and holidays. Crypto trades continuously. A closed market’s last price is not a new trade.

Technical indicators

Moving averages smooth historical price changes; oscillators describe momentum. TradingView aggregates these into technical signals for your chosen timeframe.

These signals can change and can conflict across timeframes. A buy or sell label is a technical summary, not a promise of performance or personalised advice.

The sample forecast

The dashboard’s collapsed demo fits a straight line to 60 deterministic synthetic prices using ordinary least squares. The chosen horizon extends the line by 7, 14, or 30 observations, flooring negative values at zero.

The demo does not read market data from the widgets. No accuracy evaluation or confidence interval is provided. Stock periods represent trading sessions; crypto periods represent days.

Data availability

Widgets require a connection to TradingView and may be affected by content blockers, regional restrictions, or exchange permissions. Each dashboard has an external TradingView link if embedding is unavailable.

Prices, market capitalisation, volume, and other statistics depend on the provider’s coverage. Missing values should not be interpreted as zero.

What comes next

Forecasts based on real historical prices require a separate licensed data feed, evaluation against held-out history, and explicit treatment of corporate actions and currencies. The current demo does not perform these tasks.

Use the dashboard for market exploration and compare information with authoritative sources before making financial decisions.

Market data availability and delays depend on the exchange and provider. Educational content, not investment advice.
