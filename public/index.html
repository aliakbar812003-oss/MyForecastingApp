Understand the forecast

This demo uses deterministic synthetic prices, not current market quotes. Ordinary least squares fits a straight line to 60 observations and extends it over your selected horizon. It does not account for news, fundamentals, volatility, or market shocks. No confidence interval or predictive accuracy is claimed. This is an educational illustration, not investment advice.
