If the panel is blank, open TradingView above. Data may be delayed or unavailable.

When considering a buy

Check whether the summary, trend, and momentum agree across timeframes. Read recent news and review volume. A technical buy label alone is insufficient evidence of future gains.

When considering a sell

Compare technical weakness with your original investment thesis, time horizon, and position size. Decide your exit criteria before trading. Short-term signals can reverse quickly.

Mixed signals mean uncertainty

Daily and weekly readings may disagree. Neutral is a valid outcome. Market closures, low liquidity, and delayed data can affect interpretation.

What this app does not do

No trades are placed. No guaranteed profit, probability of success, or target price is calculated from this widget. The separate sample forecast remains synthetic and is not used for these summaries.
